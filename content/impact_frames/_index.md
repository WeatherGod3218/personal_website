I draw impact frames! It started as a passion hobby, since I never really considered myself an artist. But before I knew it, I had a lot of practice in making them! It was a fun way to have a creative outlet, even though I never had good artistic ability or even good handwriting, for that matter. It first started out with making simple impact frames from a tutorial I found, and I ended up picking up a passion for it. Mainly due to the amount of creative liberties that you can have when making them.
