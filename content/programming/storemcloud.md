Store 'em Cloud can be found [here!](https://storemcloud.cs.house/)

A video detailing the application can be found [https://youtu.be/0b-tbQU94zQ/ here]!

For those who don't know much about me, I love playing video games. I also love saving clips from them. However, the issue is I DESPISE MEDAL (the clipping software) and wanted a solution so my friends could go and view my clips from these games. Store 'em cloud was my first real attempt at making an app that fulfilled scalability, and led to being the reason for getting multiple of my current jobs.

My favorite part of building this website was the architecture. Although this was done after I really locked in on system designs with the Serge team's architecture, it was a really fun application of a lot of moving pieces. Multiple different databases, auth, and so much more.
